I'm Chengdong Weng, a high school student in the United States. I'm interested in machine learning, engineering, and building things that make useful technology cheaper and more accessible.

This blog is my research notebook. Right now I'm working on a question that sits between hardware and machine learning: **how much sensor resolution does sleep-posture classification actually need?** High-resolution pressure mats can tell whether a person is lying on their back or side, which matters for pressure-ulcer prevention and patient monitoring, but the good ones are expensive. I'm studying how far you can cut the number of sensors, and when the coverage of the mat matters more than the density of sensors, before the classification breaks.

I'm doing this project under the mentorship of a postdoc researcher, and I write here to keep a public record of the work: the experiments, the dead ends, the results, and what I learn along the way.

You can find my code and projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/ChengdongWeng).
